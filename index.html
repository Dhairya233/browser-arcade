Browser Arcade

Two multiplayer board games that run in the browser. Play on one shared screen, or host a room and play from separate devices.

  • Game of Trades
    Build, trade and settle an island. 3 to 8 players.
  • Shadow Hunt
    One hidden fugitive, a team of hunters. At least 3 players.

Online rooms are peer-to-peer. The host's browser runs the game, so the host has to keep the tab open.
