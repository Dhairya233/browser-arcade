Shadow Hunt

The Phantom is loose in the city. Detectives, work together and track him down before he vanishes for good.

3 to 6 players. Pass-and-play shares one screen. Online rooms are peer-to-peer, so the host keeps their tab open.

An independent fan-made game, not affiliated with or endorsed by the makers of any commercial board game. Made by Dhairya Shah.

Same-device game

Everyone shares this screen. Between turns you will be asked to pass the device to the next player, so The Phantom's moves stay secret.

Play online

Create a room and share its link or code with friends, or join with a code you were given. Everyone needs an internet connection. The host's browser runs the game, so the host has to keep their tab open.
